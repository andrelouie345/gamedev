# ARENA SHOOTER — Game Design Document

**Team:** Names and roles not supplied. **Platform:** Windows desktop and
Android (planned), landscape. **Engine:** Godot 4.7 Standard, Compatibility
renderer.

## Pitch

An accessible first-person practice range where players sharpen their aim,
manage ammunition and advance through five increasingly demanding target
challenges.

## Genre and audience

First-person target-practice / arcade shooter for players who enjoy short,
score-driven aiming challenges. The game uses stylized, non-photorealistic
low-poly target and weapon art.

## Core mechanic and controls

Aim from the first-person camera and fire at practice targets. Hits clear a
target and contribute to hit count and precision score; headshots, bullseyes
and consecutive hits earn bonuses. Misses reduce precision score in campaign.
PC play uses WASD and mouse; desktop testing can use the on-screen touch
joystick, look area, Fire and Reload controls through mouse-to-touch emulation.
Android uses the same on-screen touch controls. R reloads; Pause opens the pause
menu.

## Goal, progression and modes

Clear each level's target/enemy goal before time expires. A timeout, defeat by
enemies or unsuccessful bot result ends the run. Successful campaign levels
unlock the next level and store the best star rating in `user://progress.cfg`.
Versus Bot offers an offline AI score race and does not change campaign unlocks.
Online is a host-authoritative ENet score race intended for two devices on the
same Wi-Fi; two-device behavior remains to be tested.

| Level | Intended challenge |
|---|---|
| 1 | Stationary silhouettes and bullseyes; learn to aim and shoot. |
| 2 | A larger target goal with slow horizontal target movement. |
| 3 | Faster, smaller moving targets in enemy waves. |
| 4 | Smaller targets, unpredictable movement and enemy attacks. |
| 5 | Fast targets, multiple waves, a boss and limited reserve ammunition. |

## Screens

Title, mode select, level select, online lobby, in-game HUD, pause, results and
credits. Results show the outcome, score, remaining time, accuracy, cleared
targets and campaign stars.

## Art, sound and assets

Simple, visibility-focused range geometry with original low-poly AK-47/arms,
human silhouette and bullseye GLBs. Textures are embedded in the generated GLBs.
Original procedural background music and game sound effects are generated by
`tools/generate_audio_assets.py`. Asset provenance is documented in
`docs/CREDITS.md`.

## Team, risks and fallback

Team member names and assigned roles were not supplied. Main risks are Android
input/layout compatibility and same-Wi-Fi network differences. Keep Campaign
and Versus Bot available offline; use the documented touch controls and
Compatibility renderer; validate Android export and networking on actual
devices before claiming support. External playtest feedback and device test
results have not been collected.
