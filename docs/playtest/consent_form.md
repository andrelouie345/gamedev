## What participation involves

The participant may be asked to try offline Campaign Levels 1–3 and, if two
compatible devices and a Wi-Fi network are available, one online match. The
facilitator may request think-aloud comments and record task completion,
attempts, usability observations and questionnaire responses under an anonymous
participant code.

Participation is voluntary. A participant may skip a question or stop at any
time without penalty. Do not collect names, phone numbers, account credentials
or unrelated personal information on the playtest forms. Explain in advance
whether any screen, audio or video recording will occur; the project does not
require recording. If recordings are proposed, obtain separate approval and
consent and document storage/deletion procedures before recording.
