# Credits

No third-party art, audio, or font assets are included in the project. The
AK-47/arms view model, silhouette and bullseye targets are original low-poly
assets generated by `tools/generate_fps_assets.py`; their small PBR textures are
embedded in the GLB files under `assets/models/`. The arena uses project-made
geometry, and the interface uses Godot UI controls. No authored music or sound
effects from third parties are included. Original synthesized background music
and sound effects are generated by `tools/generate_audio_assets.py` and stored
under `assets/audio/`.

The project team and individual asset contributors were not identified in the
materials available for this audit. Add verified creator names and any
third-party asset source and license before distribution.

If third-party assets are added later, list each asset's name, creator, source,
and license here before including it in a build.
