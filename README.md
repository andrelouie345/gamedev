# ARENA SHOOTER – ITSD 85 Lab

## About the Game

Arena Shooter is a first-person target-practice game for Godot 4.7. Aim at
stationary and moving practice targets, manage ammunition, and clear five
increasingly difficult levels. The project includes Campaign, Versus Bot and a
same-Wi-Fi ENet score race.

## How to Play

Start at the title screen, choose a mode, and select an available campaign
level. Clear the displayed target or enemy goal before the countdown expires.
Campaign clears award stars and unlock the next level. Versus Bot and online
results do not award campaign progress.

## Controls

- **PC:** WASD to move; click and drag the left virtual joystick to move; drag
  on the right half of the screen to aim; click **FIRE** to shoot; click **R**
  or press R to reload; Escape opens or resumes the pause flow.
- **Touchscreen:** Use the on-screen joystick, right-side look area, **FIRE**
  and **R** controls. Mouse-to-touch emulation is enabled for desktop testing.

## Level Progression

| Level | Challenge |
|---|---|
| 1 | Stationary practice targets and basic aiming |
| 2 | More targets with slow horizontal movement |
| 3 | Faster, smaller targets and enemy waves |
| 4 | Unpredictable movement, smaller targets and enemy attacks |
| 5 | Fast targets, multiple waves, a boss and limited reserve ammunition |

Campaign levels unlock in order after the previous level is cleared. Versus Bot
allows selecting any level without changing campaign unlocks.

## Offline and Online Modes

Campaign and Versus Bot run offline. Online mode is an ENet host-and-join score
race for two devices on the same Wi-Fi network. The joining device needs the
host's local IP address. Two-device connectivity has not been verified.

## How to Build

Open the project in Godot 4.7 Standard with the Compatibility renderer. For
Android, follow [docs/ANDROID_EXPORT.md](docs/ANDROID_EXPORT.md). No verified
Android export preset or APK is currently included.

The original low-poly GLBs and their embedded textures are under
`assets/models/`. To regenerate the models, run
`python tools/generate_fps_assets.py`. Original background music and game
effects are generated with `python tools/generate_audio_assets.py`.
Firing/reload behavior and moving-target logic are implemented in the existing
gameplay scripts; use the checklist to distinguish implementation from
interactive verification.
